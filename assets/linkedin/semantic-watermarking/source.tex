\vspace*{1.5in}
{\Large \textbf{Project Breakdown: Adversarial Dual-Stream Autoencoder (ADSA)}}
\newpage

% SLIDE 2: The Core Challenge
\thispagestyle{empty}
\vspace*{1in}
{\Huge \textbf{The Core Challenge}}\\[0.5in]
{\Large While building our deepfake defense pipeline, we hit a wall.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large We needed to watermark images cryptographically to prove provenance. But every time we ran a simulated face-swap, the watermark was completely destroyed.}\\[0.5in]

\begin{darkbox}
{\Large \textbf{The Trade-Off}}\\
If we made the watermark stronger to survive attacks, it ruined the subject's face. Human eyes are simply too sensitive to high-frequency artifacts on skin.
\end{darkbox}
\vspace*{0.5in}
{\Large We had to fundamentally rethink our approach.}
\newpage

% SLIDE 3: The Semantic-Aware Hypothesis
\thispagestyle{empty}
\vspace*{1in}
{\Huge \textbf{The Semantic-Aware Hypothesis}}\\[0.5in]
{\Large The realization was simple: Not all pixels are created equal.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large A high-variance background---like a forest, a brick wall, or busy traffic---can hide a massive amount of cryptographic noise.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large The human eye naturally ignores it.}\\[0.5in]

\begin{infobox}
{\Large Instead of watermarking the entire image equally, what if we segment the architecture based on semantic context?}
\end{infobox}
\newpage

% SLIDE 4: The Dual-Stream Architecture
\thispagestyle{empty}
\vspace*{1in}
{\Huge \textbf{The Dual-Stream Autoencoder}}\\[0.5in]
{\Large Building on the Vanilla Autoencoder concepts we've been experimenting with, we ran two parallel streams.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large In the latent space, the embedded tensor \(\widehat{z}\) is mathematically defined as:}
\[
\widehat{z}_{bg} = z_{bg} + \alpha \cdot w_{chaos}
\]
\vspace*{0.2in}
{\Large Where \(\alpha\) (the watermark strength) is heavily amplified for the background stream, completely decoupled from the object's fidelity constraints.}
\newpage

% SLIDE 5: Code Implementation
\thispagestyle{empty}
\vspace*{1in}
{\Huge \textbf{Implementation Details}}\\[0.5in]
{\Large We segmented the image using U-Net, then injected a SHA-512 chaotic spread-spectrum sequence directly into the latent feature bottlenecks.}\\[0.3in]

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, title=Semantic Partitioning]
# 1. Isolate the semantic components
object_tensor, background_tensor = semantic_segmentation(image)

# 2. Inject chaotic sequence into the latent bottlenecks
z_obj = encoder_obj(object_tensor) + delicate_watermark
z_bg = encoder_bg(background_tensor) + massive_watermark

# 3. Reconstruct and Gaussian blend
watermarked_image = blend(decoder_obj(z_obj), decoder_bg(z_bg))
\end{lstlisting}
\vspace*{0.3in}
{\Large The face gets a delicate watermark. The background gets a massive, highly robust one.}
\newpage

% SLIDE 6: The Digital Witness
\thispagestyle{empty}
\vspace*{1in}
{\Huge \textbf{The Digital Witness}}\\[0.5in]
{\Large The results proved the hypothesis.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large When the face gets swapped maliciously in a deepfake attack, the delicate face watermark is destroyed.}\\[0.3in]
{\Large But the background watermark survives perfectly.}\\[0.5in]

\begin{darkbox}
{\Large \textbf{Tamper Evidence}}\\
The surviving background acts as an immutable "Digital Witness"—cryptographically proving the image was tampered with simply because half the sequence is missing.
\end{darkbox}
